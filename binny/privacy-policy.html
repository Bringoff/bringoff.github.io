Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Artem Kalachian ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses information when you use the Binny mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

a) Photo library access

With your permission, the App accesses your photo library to show photos for sorting and to carry out actions you request (such as deleting or restoring items in the App flow). Your photos and photo content stay on your device. We do not transmit, upload, store, or share your photos on our servers as part of normal app functionality. Any iCloud Photos syncing is handled by Apple under your Apple account settings and is outside of our systems.

b) Purchase and subscription data

If you purchase Binny Pro, we receive subscription status and entitlement information from our payment and subscription partners (Apple and RevenueCat) to unlock premium features.

c) Analytics and diagnostics

We use PostHog for product analytics, session replay, and error tracking. This may include app usage events, technical diagnostics, crash/error details, device/app metadata, and interaction data needed to improve reliability and user experience. We collect this analytics data to understand feature usage, fix issues faster, and improve the App.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide and operate core app features;
  • Process and verify subscription access;
  • Maintain app performance, security, and stability;
  • Analyze usage to improve product design and functionality;
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

3. Legal Bases (EEA/UK, if applicable)

Where required by law, we process personal data based on one or more of the following:

  • Your consent (for example, photo library permission);
  • Performance of a contract (providing the App and paid features);
  • Legitimate interests (analytics, product improvement, fraud prevention);
  • Legal obligations.

4. Sharing of Information

We may share information with service providers that help us run the App, including:

  • Apple (in-app purchases and subscription billing);
  • RevenueCat (subscription and entitlement management);
  • PostHog (analytics, session replay, and error tracking).

We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security.

5. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the App, fulfill the purposes described in this policy, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Retention periods may vary by data type and legal requirements.

6. Your Rights and Choices

  • You can revoke photo permissions at any time in iOS Settings;
  • You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple account settings;
  • You may request access, correction, deletion, or portability of personal data where applicable by law;
  • You may request that we stop certain processing, subject to legal exceptions.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is rated 4+ on the App Store and may be used by a general audience. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information in a way that violates applicable law, contact us and we will review and address the request.

8. International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers under applicable data protection law.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date and provide notice where required.
